Daohechian BnB · Family hospitality business · Taiwan02 / 04

0 to 30K search impressions for a business nobody was finding

Problem

Near-zero organic visibility. Not ranking for booking-intent searches; nothing structured for AI search tools to cite either.

Insight

Not a content-volume problem. Generic hospitality copy competing against thousands of similar listings, no technical foundation underneath it.

Strategy

SEO and GEO as one workstream: fix the technical foundation, write for real search intent and local relevance, structure content so AI answer engines can cite it.

Execution

Rebuilt key pages, ran search-intent analysis, fixed technical SEO, optimised Google Business Profile, shipped content on a fixed cycle.

0 → 30K impressions~150 enquiries/month now via search.

FCS Solutions · Hospitality SaaS03 / 04

Fixing the CRM before fixing the funnel

Problem

No repeatable inbound channel, and three functions quietly working from three different definitions of a "qualified lead" — so leads that did come in barely got worked.

Strategy & execution
