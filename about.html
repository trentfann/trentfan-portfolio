ABOUT

I didn't set out to do this. I just kept noticing numbers that didn't add up.

SaaS, ecommerce, martech, and hospitality — Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore, Australia. Independent since January 2026.

I studied marketing management in Bangkok, took my first ecommerce job at a hotel group because it was hiring, and ended up being the person who kept asking why the traffic numbers didn't match what everyone assumed. That's stayed true for nine years and four countries — I get curious about the number that doesn't make sense, and I don't really stop until I understand why.

What I actually enjoy is the unglamorous part: untangling a CRM three teams have quietly stopped trusting, figuring out why a referral program isn't converting, building the first version of something — a webinar program, an SEO practice, a paid ads account — that didn't exist before I showed up. I've done that inside SaaS companies, ecommerce brands, and a hotel group in Bangkok, which is a strange enough range that I've stopped assuming any one playbook works everywhere.

Clients tend to keep me around longer than a single project. Paul at SparkyMall and I have worked together for six years now, across two different chapters of his business. I think that's because I'll tell someone when I think they're solving the wrong problem, and then I stay long enough to be wrong about something myself and fix it.
