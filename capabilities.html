A DISTINCTION WORTH MAKING

Newsletter and creator-led businesses aren't running email marketing the way an ecommerce brand does. For a content creator, an independent publisher, or a media business, the email list isn't a communication channel bolted onto the product — it is the business asset that acquisition, retention, and revenue all run through.

EMAIL AS A CHANNEL
Ecommerce, SaaS: one acquisition and retention tool among several, measured against other channels.
EMAIL AS THE BUSINESS
Newsletters, publishers, creators: the audience asset itself — growth strategy has to start there, not treat it as a line item.
